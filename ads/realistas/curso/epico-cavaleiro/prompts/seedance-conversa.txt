At dusk, beside the one torch on a tower wall, a spent knight asks an unarmed younger man for his secret, and the younger man answers without fear.

The walk on top of a castle wall, just after sunset, in a cold wind. At the right it ends against a square tower: one flat face of pale rough limestone, broader and taller than any frame. The younger man stands with his back to it; it is the whole background behind him.
